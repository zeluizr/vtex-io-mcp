## Summary

Enabling the performance flag "Lazy rendering of search results and facets" in `/admin/cms/store` affects the PLP product grid, causing products to break the display structure of the showcase.

## Simulation

The problem only occurs when the page is accessed directly or reloaded (F5). Navigation via the menu (SPA navigation) does not break the display.

## Workaround

No workaround available.
