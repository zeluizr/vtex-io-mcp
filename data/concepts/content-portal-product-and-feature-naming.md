## Objective

Establish a clear, consistent, and meaningful naming strategy for VTEX products, features, and projects to enhance brand identity, customer understanding, and market positioning.

Why have a naming process?

- **Consistency:** Ensures all names align with the brand's identity and values.
- **Clarity:** Helps customers easily understand what each product, feature, or module does.
- **Professionalism:** Demonstrates a well-organized and thoughtful approach.
- **Market Positioning:** Differentiates VTEX products from competitors.
- **Global Relevance:** Ensures names are appropriate and understandable across different languages and cultures.
- **Efficiency:** Streamlines the naming process, saving time and resources.
- **Marketing and SEO:** Improves searchability and marketing effectiveness.

When does this process apply?

- Launching new products
- Developing features and modules
- Updating existing names
- Adapting names for different regions and languages

**The sooner, the better.** Ideally, run this process at least 2 months before rolling out to closed beta.

## General guidelines

Names must be:

- **Meaningful:** Conveys relevant meaning and fits within the industry.
- **Unique:** Differentiates from competitors and is not too generic.
- **Memorable:** Easy to remember and recognize.
- **Easy to Spell:** Should be spelled as it is pronounced.
- **Easy to Pronounce:** Should be pronounced as it is spelled.
- **Brief:** Limited to one or two words, max of three, or a compound word.
- **Global Relevance:** Easy to understand in different languages and cultures.
- **SEO Friendly:** Names should be searchable and rankable.

## Translation rules

- **Products:** Keep in English (examples: Sales App, Live Shopping, FastStore).
- **VTEX Admin Modules:** Translated (examples: Overview / Visão geral, Shipping / Envio).
- **Permission Roles:** Not translated (examples: Owner (Admin Super), VTEX IO Admin).
- **Features:** Translated (example: Operational capacity / Capacidade operacional).
- **Apps:** Some are translated, some are not (examples: Wishlist / Lista de desejos, Customer Credit).

## Capitalization

Use title case for titles in English, and sentence case in Portuguese and Spanish.

## Character limit

- Modules - 18 characters
- Sections - 16 characters
- Subsections - 21 characters

## What to avoid

- Don't use verbs. For example, use "Available Sellers" instead of "Find Sellers."
- Don't use punctuation.
- Don't use personal pronouns.
- Avoid redundancy or repeat unnecessary terms.
- Avoid using abbreviations unless they are widely recognized, such as "B2B" or "SKU."

## Streamlined process for simpler features

1. Fill a workflow in the #terminology Slack group.
2. Necessity check: Determine if a new name is truly necessary.
3. Generate name ideas: Analyze competitors, create a short list, check alignment with VTEX's strategy.
4. Team discussion: Validate names with requester, Localization and Product Marketing teams.
5. Implementation: Review feedback, decide, socialize, and ensure consistent usage.
