You can configure one of your store's banners to redirect users to a page of your choosing. Link the banner to any internal or external URL using the Site Editor.

## Internal URLs

1. In the VTEX Admin, access **Storefront > Site Editor**.
2. Select a **Carousel** block and choose the banner whose content you want to edit.
3. In the `Internal URL` field, copy and paste the internal URL of your choosing.
4. Click on `APPLY` > `SAVE`.

> The *Banner target page* and *Params* fields have been deprecated. The data filled in the Internal URL field is the only data taken into account for the internal route.

## External URLs

1. In the VTEX Admin, access **Storefront > Site Editor**.
2. Select a **Carousel** block and choose the banner whose content you want to edit.
3. Click the **External Route** toggle.
4. In the `URL (external)` field, copy and paste the external URL.
5. Click on `APPLY` > `SAVE`.

## Best practices

- Use internal URLs for pages within your VTEX store to ensure proper tracking and analytics.
- Use external URLs for links to social media, partner websites, or other external resources.
- Always test banner links after configuration to ensure they redirect correctly.
- Consider using UTM parameters on external URLs for campaign tracking.
