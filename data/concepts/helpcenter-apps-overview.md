A VTEX app extends the functionalities of the VTEX platform, helping merchants create comprehensive experiences for different layers of digital commerce, such as:

- Marketing and affiliate programs
- Reviews, ratings, and analytics
- Customer support and notifications
- Marketplaces and channels
- Finance and taxes
- Logistics and productivity
- Privacy and security
- Store design

Both first-party and third-party apps are available for download at the [VTEX App Store](https://apps.vtex.com/), and they can be either free or paid.

> If you are a developer, check the [Developer Portal](https://developers.vtex.com) for more information on how to develop your own VTEX apps.

The Apps menu allows merchants to download new apps directly from the App Store and manage installed apps without leaving the VTEX Admin.

## Apps management

The Apps management section allows you to:

- Check the complete list of apps available in the App Store.
- Install new apps (Admin apps or otherwise) in your account.
- Configure settings for the installed apps.
- Uninstall an app.

Pages in this section:

- **App Store**: Browse and install apps.
- **My Apps**: View and manage currently installed apps.

## Installed Apps

The Installed Apps section includes the configuration settings for the Admin apps installed in your account. Pages in this section depend on the apps installed.

## Apps in the redesigned Admin

The new design system provides a clean, improved, and unified experience. Third-party apps are recommended to adapt to the new design system and the updated architecture of building apps for `admin@4.x` standards.
